The Founding

In April 2026, Manoj Kedia and Micky Kedia founded this Trust — not as an investment vehicle, but as a promise. A pledge that whatever the family builds from here on, it builds together, and holds in trust for the generations who haven't been born yet.

Vision

A family that stays a family — on purpose, not by accident.

As the years pass and branches spread across cities and lives get busier, this Trust exists to be the quiet thread that keeps everyone in one story.

Mission
  • Stand by any family member through genuine need.
  • Steward what the family builds, prudently and transparently.
  • Keep the family's history, faces, and stories from fading.
  • Give every generation a reason to gather, in person, every year.
Why the Trust Exists

Five commitments, held in perpetuity.

Everything the Trust does traces back to one of these. Nothing more, nothing less.

01

Family, first

Financial support to any member of the family in a moment of genuine need — decided with judgement, not formula.

02

Preserve & grow

What the Trust holds is preserved and grown through disciplined, prudent, lawful stewardship — never speculation.

03

Built to outlast us

Continuity and financial security carried forward across generations, not just for those alive today.

04

Reasons to gather

An annual family gathering, and a standing commitment to mark birthdays, anniversaries, and family occasions.

05

Whatever it lawfully takes

The Trustees may act on anything reasonably in service of the above — nothing here is meant to be a cage.

∞

Governed, not improvised

Every one of these commitments is exercised strictly within the Trust Deed and Indian law.

How It Runs

Discipline is the whole point.

A family trust only means something if it can be trusted. These are the rules that make sure of it.

  • 01

    Unanimous, always

    Every decision on investment, distribution, or policy requires 100% consent of all Trustees — no majority rule, no tie-breaks.

  • 02

    The Settlors' word is final

    For as long as either Settlor is alive, their direction binds the Trustees completely. The Trustees act, they don't overrule.

  • 03

    Irrevocable, in time

    Once both Settlors have passed, the Trust becomes absolute and irrevocable — and continues in perpetuity for the family.

  • 04

    Kept in the family

    Trustees are replaced only from among family members, with at least two in office at all times, stepping down at sixty.

  • 05

    Meets every year, on record

    At least one formal meeting annually, with minutes signed by every Trustee — the record is the Trust's memory.

Where We Come From

Family Heritage

What began with Govind Prasad Kedia and Sita Devi Kedia has, over the decades, grown into a wide family of children, grandchildren, and now great-grandchildren, each carrying the same name forward in their own way.

This Trust is the family's way of formalising something that was always true informally — that no one in this family stands alone, and that the ties between us are worth actively protecting, not just hoping for.

Investment Philosophy

How the Trust is managed.

Invested, not idle

Funds are held primarily through a small, disciplined set of mutual fund SWPs — never scattered across too many schemes.

Withdrawals stay conservative

Trustees aim to keep withdrawals within a sustainable ceiling, reviewed periodically — nothing is promised or guaranteed.

Surplus, put to work

Spare balances may sit in liquid, redeemable instruments like ETFs, ready when the family actually needs them.

Accountable on paper

Proper books, a PAN in the Trust's name, and audits by a Chartered Accountant when required.
